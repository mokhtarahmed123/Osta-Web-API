
# Use Cases

## Osta Plus Use Cases

---

# 1. Customer Use Cases

### UC-001 Register Account

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
A new customer creates an account to access the platform.

---

### UC-002 Login

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
A customer logs into the platform using their registered credentials.

---

### UC-003 Update Profile

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
The customer updates their personal information.

---

### UC-004 Search for Technicians

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Search technicians by category, location, availability, rating, or service.

---

### UC-005 View Technician Profile

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
View technician information, ratings, completed jobs, and available services.

---

### UC-006 Create Booking

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Book a technician for a specific service and preferred appointment time.

---

### UC-007 Cancel Booking

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Cancel an existing booking before the technician starts the service.

---

### UC-008 Track Booking

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Monitor the current booking status.

---

### UC-009 Make Payment

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Pay for completed services using supported payment methods.

---

### UC-010 Submit Review

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
Rate the technician and write a review after service completion.

---

### UC-011 View Booking History

**Actor:** Customer

**Description:**
View all previous bookings and their statuses.
